# Agent Brief — Marketing & GTM Foundation

> Paste into a GTM/content agent (web research helpful).

## Product context
**AI Sales Coach** — real-time in-call coaching for the **individual sales rep** (self-serve,
**pay-as-you-go credits**). Live cues during any call + a coaching report after + practice against
an AI persona of the prospect. A desktop app the rep installs themselves. Backend live at
`sales-coach.shipdeck.dev`. Market data + positioning already researched in `product/research.md`
and `product/realtime-plan.md` (key facts: real-time coaching is absent at the SMB/individual price
point; incumbents Gong/Chorus are expensive manager tools; generic notetakers are post-call only;
the product risk is cue *relevance* / alert fatigue).

**Hard rules:** never use "NEPQ"/"Jeremy Miner" (describe coaching generically); never claim
"invisible on screen-share"; ICP = the individual rep, bottom-up.

## Your role & task
Produce the marketing foundation:
1. **Positioning + messaging** — one positioning statement, 3–5 value props, hero/subhead options,
   and objection handling — all for the solo rep (not managers).
2. **Competitive framing** — vs Gong/Chorus (manager tools, expensive, annual) and vs notetakers
   (post-call only). Our wedge: *for the rep, live, pay-as-you-go.*
3. **Pricing-page copy** — pay-as-you-go credits; anchor on value ("one closed deal pays for
   months"); explain credits simply.
4. **Channel plan** — where individual reps actually are (r/sales, sales LinkedIn, sales
   YouTube/TikTok, Discords/communities, cold-outreach circles); 3 prioritized channels + content
   angles (live-cue demo clips, before/after a call, "close more on your next call").
5. **Launch sequence** — waitlist → private beta → launch; the email/lifecycle copy for each.

## Deliverable / Return
Write `marketing-foundation.md` (all five). Return: the positioning statement + the top 3 channels.
